Planning the onboard electronics

A proposed Raspberry Pi and Blue Robotics control architecture under a prototype budget.

Blog / Entry

Architecture

The intended architecture uses a Raspberry Pi as the onboard computer and a Blue Robotics controller. The control and compute hardware must fit within the mechanical design, along with power electronics, wiring and protected enclosures.

Budget and trade-offs

I set an approximate $800 prototype budget during the initial electronics planning. That is a target, not a documented purchase total. It will guide the bill of materials and prioritization of essential functions.

Verification gates

Exact component models, wiring, enclosure geometry, power budget and propulsion configuration still need confirmation. Bench and leak testing must precede any powered underwater trials.
