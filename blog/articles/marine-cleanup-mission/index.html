Prototype first: an underwater robot for marine cleanup

The environmental purpose behind Stingray and the path from prototype to a potential cleanup service.

Blog / Entry

Mission

Stingray begins with a simple ambition: develop robotic tools that can contribute to a cleaner marine environment. An eventual service model, rather than selling robots, is the direction I am exploring.

Prototype before business

The immediate goal is to build and test a functioning prototype. A capable vehicle, a safe deployment method and a measurable debris-recovery task must come before any commercial claims.

Longer-term exploration

A separate ocean-exploration submersible is a longer-term idea. For now, the engineering journal will record experiments, successes, setbacks and decisions as the prototype develops.
