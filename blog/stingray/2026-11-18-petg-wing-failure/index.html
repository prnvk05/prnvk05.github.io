Failed prints: PETG wing segment

Photographic evidence of a rejected regular-PETG wing print, with defects concentrated around supported features.

Blog / Stingray / Entry

Activity

Documented rejected Stingray wing-segment prints in regular PETG. The date is the date of this log; exact print dates and slicer profiles are not yet confirmed.

Observations

A mostly formed panel with mounting tabs is visible, alongside a rough supported edge, loose filament and incomplete-looking local geometry. Several tree-support branches are detached, but the photographs cannot establish whether they broke during printing or afterward.

Working hypothesis

The support and overhang region is the first area to investigate. Support contact, motion, bridging or extrusion may be involved; none is confirmed. This regular-PETG failure is distinct from earlier PETG-CF print-quality issues.

Decision and next experiment

Reject the affected parts for assembly. Capture the exact segment identifier, nozzle, orientation and slicer settings. Test representative wing geometry with one variable changed at a time, and record print time, material waste and dimensional results.
